The Club · Since 1991

A place that grows with you.

Two destinations in Dik el-Mehdi: the Indoor Club, open all year, and Leisure Island, our outdoor resort for summer.

Our Story

A legacy of movement. Since 1991.

Founded in 1991, the club has spent more than three decades doing the same simple thing: giving families a place to move, swim, rest and celebrate. It has grown with them, and it keeps growing.

Our Philosophy

More than a club.

Four ideas we come back to in everything we build.

Movement

Every body, every age, every level, from a first class to a first race.

Wellness

Recovery and rest matter as much as effort.

Leisure

Long summer days, good food and time together.

Community

The people who make the club what it is.

Our Facilities

One club, two destinations.

Ten green acres terraced into the hillside, wrapped in pine forest with views to the sea.

Indoor · all year

The Indoor Club

  • Gym, strength & cardio
  • Hybrid Athlete Performance Center
  • Heated 25 m indoor pool
  • Sauna, steam & jacuzzi
  • Studios for group classes
Outdoor · summer

Leisure Island

  • Outdoor lap pool
  • Slides, leisure & kids' pools
  • Football & basketball
  • Grill House & bars
  • Wedding garden & terraces
