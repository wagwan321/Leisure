Our Story

A legacy of movement. Since 1991.

Founded in 1991, the club has spent more than three decades doing the same simple thing: giving families a place to move, swim, rest and celebrate. It has grown with them, and it keeps growing.

Archive4:5

The club, 1990sFrom the club's own archive. Scan, don't retouch.

Same view, todayRe-shot from the archive angle.

  1. 1991

    The club opens

    A new club in the hills of Park Tamish, minutes from Champville.

  2. —

    Leisure Island

    The outdoor water resort: Olympic pool, slides and gardens.

  3. —

    A generation of swimmers

    The Learn to Swim school and Swim Club take shape.

  4. 2026

    The newest chapter

    The Hybrid Athlete Performance Center: Lebanon's first HYROX gym.

Our Facilities

One club, two destinations.

Ten green acres terraced into the hillside, wrapped in pine forest with views to the sea.

Indoor · all year

The Indoor Club

  • Gym, strength & cardio
  • Hybrid Athlete Performance Center
  • Heated 25 m indoor pool
  • Sauna, steam & jacuzzi
  • Studios for group classes
Outdoor · summer

Leisure Island

  • Olympic 50 m lap pool
  • Slides, leisure & kids' pools
  • Football & basketball
  • Grill House & bars
  • Wedding garden & terraces
