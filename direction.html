01 — What we heard

Not another gym website. A destination for movement, wellness, leisure and community, which should make people want to visit before they ever have. It should feel timeless, premium, minimal and distinctive, emotionally engaging but clear, and built to grow.

Target blend: 50% architectural minimalism, 30% premium wellness and lifestyle, 20% athletic energy. Studied, never imitated: Aman (restraint, atmosphere), Aesop (editorial discipline), Equinox (athletic confidence), Six Senses (emotional storytelling) and Technogym (performance).

Accessibility note

The brief's muted gray #858585 reaches only 3.4:1 on the off-white, so we keep it for large text. Small supporting text uses #6B6A66 (5:1, which passes WCAG AA). Red text on light is darkened to #C8141C.

04 — Leisure Island accent

Per the owner's note, Leisure Island is fun and bright. It keeps the same type, grid and restraint, but switches to a sunlit flat palette wherever the island appears. This works with either direction. We still use no gradients, as the brief asks.

06 — Motion options

The site now uses Expressive by default, built from what the reference sites actually do while you scroll. Equinox: smooth inertial scrolling and a framed film. Aman and Six Senses: horizontal story rows, statements that come alive as you read, and quiet parallax. Technogym: performance film that expands to fill the screen. Every movement is slow and eased, never flashy, so it stays within the brief's "no aggressive animation" (§03). Press Play on each card. All of it switches off for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion, and the other two levels are still one click away under Design options.

More than a club.Movement. Wellness. Leisure. Community.

No animation. Content is simply there. It is the most architectural option, and it is also what reduced-motion visitors get.

  2. Heritage"A legacy of movement. Since 1991." A short story, a then-and-now photo pair and four facts.

  6. CommunityRun Club, Swim Club, events, achievements and the latest News & Stories.
  7. Leisure IslandThe bright sub-identity: an editable season status, six experiences and a prominent "Plan a celebration" route.
  8. VisitLocation, hours, reception, WhatsApp, socials, a map and a three-field inquiry routed to the right team.

09 — Still to confirm with the club

From the meeting sheet, plus the facts the prototype marks "to confirm". The brief says to publish only confirmed current offerings, so none of these go live until the club signs them off.

Meeting decisions still open

  1. Which audiences and inquiries come first at launch? (The note on the sheet brackets this with prices, so please confirm.)
  2. Which photography and video will be commissioned, and when? (See the shot list above.)
  3. Who maintains content and schedules day to day?
  4. Timeline, milestones, budget and acceptance criteria.
  5. Is Expressive motion the right amount? (Palette decided: the brief's table.)

Facts to verify

  • Membership prices for every plan, which are now going online
  • Hybrid Center access rules: what is free on the floor, and what is a booked class
  • Current class timetable, with coaches
  • Indoor opening hours, and Leisure Island season dates for 2027
  • Which number is the WhatsApp line, and who receives each type of inquiry
  • The heritage story: a few true sentences from the founders
