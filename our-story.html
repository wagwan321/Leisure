Then · 1991 Now · 2026

Timeline

1991The Leisure Club
  1. 1991

    Where it begins

    The Leisure Club opens in Dik el-Mehdi, North Metn. Its promise, still used today, is a family reward.

    Archive3:2

    Opening yearsFrom the club's own archive.

  2. 1990s – 2000s

    The founding years

    The first pools, the first members, the first summers. This chapter will be written with the founders and the club's archive.

  3. 2010

    A country club and wedding venue

    Indoors, a health club with gym, pool and spa, sauna and steam, aerobics, dance and martial arts. Outdoors, Leisure Island: wedding gardens, three pools, water slides, a playground, acro-branching and a flying fox.

    Archive3:2

    Leisure Island, early 2010sSlides, gardens and the flying fox.

  4. 2011

    A bigger pool takes shape

    The club announces an Olympic pool under construction on Leisure Island.

  5. 2016

    A full week of programmes

    Learn to Swim, summer camp, karate, a football court, ping pong and an aerobic challenge fill the club's week.

  6. 2017

    Swimming for the French Bac

    The pool trains students for the French Baccalaureate's swimming and lifesaving test, alongside water therapy, dance and the spa.

  7. 2018

    New energy

    Boxing and Zumba join the timetable.

  8. 2021

    The Bianco Bar and a new kids' programme

    Evenings gain the Bianco Bar, and children a full programme: football, Zumba, taekwondo, gymnastics and Learn to Swim.

    Archive3:2

    Bianco BarGarden lights, an evening in summer.

  9. Today

    Island life grows

    The Grill House, the Escape bungalows and The Pilates Club extend what a day at the club can be.

  10. 2026

    The newest chapter

    The club turns 35, and the Hybrid Athlete Performance Center takes shape as Lebanon's first HYROX gym.
