The newest chapter

Hybrid Athlete Performance Center.

Lebanon's first HYROX gym

Built for athletes who want to be strong, fast and durable at the same time, and for anyone curious enough to find out what they're capable of.

Film · hero loop21:9

The Center in motionWide establishing shot, then race-format movement: run, sled, ski-erg, wall-balls. Real athletes, natural sound optional.

Philosophy

Strength without endurance is half an athlete. Endurance without strength is the other half. Here, you train both.

Run

Engine first. Every session comes back to running.

Lift

Strength that carries over: pushing, pulling, carrying.

Repeat

Race simulation, so effort under fatigue feels familiar.

Recover

Sauna, steam and jacuzzi a few steps away.
