№ 02The Pools

Three ways into the water.

i. Out in the sun

The lap pool & the slides

Swim laps in the big pool, then race down the twisting yellow slides into the leisure pool below. Loungers and umbrellas hold the shore all day.

SeasonMay — September
SlidesThree, twisting
ii. All year round

The heated indoor pool

When the mountain air cools, the fun moves indoors. Warm water, marble columns, and home to our learn-to-swim school — first floats to full laps.

SeasonAll year
Swim schoolAll ages & levels
iii. Dry land

Pitch, terraces & camps

A full-size football pitch for matches and summer camps, green terraces for the grandparents, and pine shade for everyone in between.

PitchFull-size turf
CampsEvery summer

№ 05Weddings & Events
Say yes among the pines, celebrate by the water.

For three decades the club has hosted weddings, baptisms and birthdays on its terraces — mountain air, garden lights, and a team that has seen a thousand toasts.

  • Open-air terraces with mountain views
  • The Bianco Bar for receptions & after-parties
  • Catering, music & planning handled in-house
