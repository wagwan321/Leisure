    № 03The Water

    Four ways into the blue.

    i. The serious end

    The Olympic lap pool

    Fifty metres of proper swimming under the mountain sky — lap lanes by morning, families by noon, and lit a deep glowing blue after dark.

    LengthOlympic, 8 lanes
    SeasonMay — September
    ii. The fast way down

    The slides & leisure pool

    Race down the twisting yellow and blue slides into the big free-form pool, then drift to the shallow end while the loungers hold the shore all day.

    SlidesThree, twisting
    MoodPure summer

    Kids' pools & splash pad

    Mushroom fountains, rainbow arches and a shark that never bites — shallow, sunny and made for first swims and long happy afternoons.

    № 06Eat & Drink

    From live BBQ to
    frozen margaritas.

    Move, eat, relax, repeat. Three kitchens and bars keep the whole club fed — from shawarma off the spit to crepes by the kids' pool, without ever leaving your lounger.

    Poolside restaurant

    The Grill House

    Live BBQ off the coals — shawarma, taouk, kabab and lamb chops — alongside Angus burgers, wraps, pizzas and a proper kids' menu. Breakfast tray for two until noon, $25.

    Sip & swim

    The Pool Bar

    Frozen margaritas, pina coladas and passion-fruit mojitos, wine from Ksara and Carel, any shot $5 — as the pines go dark the music comes up.

    Sweet moments

    Leisure Treats

    Crepes, fresh juices, cotton candy and the $5 Island Crepe — a sweet break for the whole family.

    After dark

    Bianco Bar

    Dinner turns into dancing under the garden lights — and the whole bar is bookable for your own occasion.

    Book an evening
    Feed a crowd

    Catering

    Canapés, charcuterie, quiches and full mains — the Grill House caters your event, on the grounds or off. Pre-order a must.
