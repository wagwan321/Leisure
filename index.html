The Resort

One Address, Endless Ways to Play

Spread across the green hills of Deir Tamish, The Leisure Club brings together water, sport and wellness for the whole family — in one welcoming place.

Outdoor Pools & Slides
Splash & Play

Water Slides & Sun-Soaked Pools

Race down our twisting slides, dive into the big outdoor pool, or stretch out on a lounger under the Lebanese sun. It's the summer day your family looks forward to all year.

  • Multiple water slides for kids & thrill-seekers
  • Large outdoor pool plus a shallow kids' pool
  • Shaded sun-loungers & umbrellas all around
Indoor Heated Pool
All Year Round

An Indoor Pool for Every Season

When the mountain air turns cool, our warm indoor pool keeps the fun going. It's also home to our certified learn-to-swim program for all ages and levels.

  • Heated indoor pool open through the winter
  • Learn-to-swim classes from first floats to laps
  • Spa & health-club facilities to unwind
